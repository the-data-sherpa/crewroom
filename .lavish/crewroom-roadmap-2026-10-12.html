Corrections become enforceable checks

an evidence-linked correction record that groups recurring mistakes from retained steering, review and repair history, then identifies the strongest practical remedy: domain/API design, types, static analysis, behavioral tests or guidance requiring judgment. A recurring class cites at least two observed occurrences. Record why the selected layer fits and whether an existing check already covers it.

  • Trace one recurring mistake to at least two real occurrences and a justified enforcement layer.
  • Prove an existing or improved check rejects the historical bad case and accepts legitimate behavior.
  • Run a bounded mutation trial on one small deterministic module; record equivalent survivors and runtime cost.
Completion evidence
  • Trace one recurring mistake from its recorded occurrences through a justified remedy to proof that the check rejects a real historical bad case and accepts legitimate behavior. Reuse existing enforcement if it already works; do not invent a new defect or duplicate a check to complete the slice.
  • A source reference that is missing, or an isolated one-off correction, cannot be presented as an evidenced recurring class. A finding requiring judgment remains a proposal rather than an asserted mechanical guarantee.
  • The mutation trial records its module/source revision, tool configuration, exercised mutations, outcomes and runtime. Any assertion improvement is verified against both the original behavior and the relevant mutation. A trial that finds no useful gap may complete with that bounded result.
  • Proposal review preserves S7's operator decision, provenance and immutable admitted assignments. Structural work follows the existing operator queue and never bypasses the human merge gate.

Compare instruction revisions before adoption

an operator-started comparison for one proposed role, principle or playbook revision against its current revision. Reuse the relevant editor/proposal surface for starting a bounded evaluation and opening its result; add no separate fleet or automatic promotion workflow. Begin with one real proposed revision while using the same comparison contract for all three instruction kinds.

  • Compare current and proposed role, principle or playbook instructions under identical recorded settings.
  • Use at least four cases and two attempts per case per variant: a minimum of 16 attempts for one comparison.
  • Keep worker prompts free of the scoring rubric; retain evidence, interventions, repairs, time and available usage.
  • Include a bounded live-agent comparison, recovery and truthful unfinished results; adoption remains an operator decision.
Completion evidence
  • Old and proposed instructions execute the same cases with the recorded settings and repetitions. Results retain each attempt and a baseline comparison; a single successful run does not establish improvement.
  • Deterministic fixtures prove that false completion, stale evidence and wrong-surface verification are detected. A separate bounded run through real supervised agents and a live model supplies behavioral evidence; scripted replies alone cannot complete that live acceptance case.
  • Pause, interruption, restart and unavailable-provider cases preserve completed results and mark unfinished attempts accurately. Resumption uses the existing supervision/ownership contract without duplicating writers or external effects. Any new runner tools pass a real-runner contract check.
  • The operator can inspect results and adopt or reject through the existing decision path. Evaluation completion never adopts a revision, changes a current assignment, satisfies coding readiness or becomes a mandatory gate for every ordinary configuration edit.

What earns the next MVP claim

Required by MH2–MH6

  • Real contract tests for OMP, Codex and Claude Code: tools, messages, observed identity, interruption and recovery.
  • Select an eligible model through each verified adapter; preserve existing OMP history and refuse unavailable or incompatible routes truthfully.
  • A current plan proceeds without redundant scoping; an unplanned job saves an accepted plan before execution.
  • A real backend job and a UI job use suitable teams. The navigator challenges work, documentation finishes before candidate freeze, and final review stays independent.
  • Interrupted work resumes without duplicate writers or reused stale evidence.

Constraints carried forward

One executing coding ticket.
Three compatible specialists plus the coordinator.
One writer per owned worktree.
Human final review and merge.
